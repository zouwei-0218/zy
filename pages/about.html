Company Profile

Welcome to Foshan ZY Furniture, your trusted partner in comprehensive F&B space solutions. We specialize in providing end-to-end services for a diverse range of hospitality venues, including restaurants, cafes, fast-food chains, dessert shops, bars, and cigar lounges. Our core expertise seamlessly integrates strategic 2D floor planning, photorealistic 3D rendering, and one-stop matching furniture sourcing. Our mission is to bridge the gap between your creative vision and practical execution.
